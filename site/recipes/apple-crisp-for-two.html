Apple Crisp for Two

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

Warm cinnamon apples with a buttery oat topping, baked in two ramekins or a small dish. Fall comfort in a small batch.

Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Total40 min
Serves2

All the cozy flavor of a big apple crisp, made in a small batch for two. Two apples are tossed with cinnamon and baked under a crunchy, buttery oat topping.

Serve warm with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Apple Crisp for Two

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 30 min · Total: 40 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

  • For the filling
  • For the topping

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F. Butter two 8-ounce ramekins or a small baking dish.
  2. Toss the apples with the sugar, flour, cinnamon, and lemon juice. Divide between the ramekins.
  3. Mix the oats, flour, brown sugar, and salt. Rub in the cold butter with your fingertips until crumbly.
  4. Sprinkle the topping over the apples.
  5. Set on a baking sheet and bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until golden and bubbling. Cool for 5 minutes before serving.

Tips for success

  • Use a mix of tart and sweet apples, such as Granny Smith and Honeycrisp.
  • Add a tablespoon of chopped pecans to the topping for crunch.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat in the microwave.
