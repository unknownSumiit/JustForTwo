Honey Garlic Salmon for Two

Two salmon fillets baked with a sticky honey garlic glaze in 15 minutes. Easy, healthy, and full of flavor.

Cook14 min

Two salmon fillets, a quick four-ingredient glaze, and 15 minutes in the oven: that is all it takes for this easy, elegant dinner.

Serve with rice and a green vegetable, and dinner is done.

Honey Garlic Salmon for Two

Prep: 5 min · Cook: 14 min · Total: 19 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F and line a small baking sheet with foil or parchment.
  2. Place the salmon skin side down and season lightly with salt and pepper.
  3. Whisk the honey, soy sauce, lemon juice, garlic, and oil. Spoon most of it over the salmon.
  4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes, until the salmon flakes easily and reaches 145°F.
  5. Spoon the remaining glaze over the top and sprinkle with green onion.

Tips for success

  • For a caramelized top, broil for the last minute, watching closely.
  • Leftover salmon is delicious flaked over a salad the next day.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 2 days.
