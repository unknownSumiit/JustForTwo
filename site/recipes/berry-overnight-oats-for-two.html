Berry Overnight Oats for Two

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

Creamy no-cook overnight oats with yogurt, berries, and a drizzle of honey. Two jars, five minutes of work.

Prep5 min
Chill6 hr
Total6 hr 5 min
Serves2
Jump to Recipe

Overnight oats are a gift to your future self. Stir everything into two jars before bed, and breakfast is waiting in the refrigerator in the morning.

They are filling, naturally sweet, and easy to vary with whatever fruit you have.

Berry Overnight Oats for Two

Prep: 5 min · Chill: 6 hr · Total: 6 hr 5 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Divide the oats, milk, yogurt, chia seeds, honey, vanilla, and cinnamon between two jars. Stir well.
  2. Cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours or overnight.
  3. In the morning, stir, add a splash of milk if needed, and top with the berries and nuts.

Tips for success

  • Frozen berries work well. Add them the night before and they will thaw into a lovely syrup.
  • Warm the oats in the microwave for 1 to 2 minutes if you prefer a hot breakfast.

Storing and reheating

Keep covered in the refrigerator for up to 4 days.
