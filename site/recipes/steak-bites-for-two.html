Garlic Butter Steak Bites for Two

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

Seared sirloin steak bites tossed in garlic herb butter, ready in 15 minutes. Steakhouse flavor for two.

Prep5 min
Cook8 min
Total13 min
Serves2
Jump to Recipe

When you want something special without much effort, these steak bites deliver. Cubes of sirloin sear in a hot skillet and get tossed in sizzling garlic butter.

Serve them with a baked potato and a salad for a steakhouse dinner at home.

Garlic Butter Steak Bites for Two

Prep: 5 min · Cook: 8 min · Total: 13 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Pat the steak dry and season with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the oil in a cast-iron or heavy skillet over high heat until shimmering.
  3. Add the steak in a single layer and sear without moving for 2 minutes. Turn and cook 1 to 2 minutes more, until browned.
  4. Reduce the heat to low, add the butter and garlic, and toss for 1 minute until fragrant. Sprinkle with parsley and serve.

Tips for success

  • The USDA recommends cooking steak to 145°F, then resting it for 3 minutes. Use an instant-read thermometer.
  • Do not crowd the pan, or the steak will steam instead of browning.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat briefly in a hot skillet.
