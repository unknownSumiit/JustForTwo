Soft Scrambled Eggs on Buttered Toast

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

Creamy, tender scrambled eggs cooked low and slow, piled on buttered toast. A simple, perfect breakfast for two.

Prep5 min
Cook8 min
Total13 min
Serves2
Jump to Recipe

Great scrambled eggs are all about gentle heat. Cooked slowly in butter and stirred often, they turn soft, creamy, and tender instead of dry and rubbery.

Serve them on thick slices of buttered toast with a sprinkle of chives and a few cherry tomatoes on the side.

Soft Scrambled Eggs on Buttered Toast

Prep: 5 min · Cook: 8 min · Total: 13 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. In a bowl, whisk the eggs, milk, salt, and pepper until completely smooth.
  2. Start toasting the bread.
  3. Melt the butter in a nonstick skillet over medium-low heat.
  4. Pour in the eggs. Let them sit for 20 seconds, then slowly push them around the pan with a silicone spatula, forming soft curds.
  5. Keep stirring gently. When the eggs are mostly set but still look slightly glossy, about 3 to 4 minutes, remove the pan from the heat. They will finish cooking in the pan’s warmth.
  6. Butter the toast, spoon the eggs on top, and sprinkle with chives.

Tips for success

  • Take the eggs off the heat a moment before they look done. That is the secret to creamy eggs.
  • Stir in a tablespoon of cream cheese or shredded cheddar at the end for extra richness.

Storing and reheating

Scrambled eggs are best enjoyed right away. Refrigerate leftovers for up to 2 days and reheat gently.
