Garlic Shrimp and Tomato Skillet

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

Juicy shrimp simmered with cherry tomatoes, garlic, and herbs in one skillet. Serve with crusty bread for dipping.

Prep10 min
Cook12 min
Total22 min
Serves2
Jump to Recipe

This one-skillet dinner is bursting with flavor: shrimp and sweet cherry tomatoes simmer together in garlicky olive oil until the tomatoes collapse into a quick sauce.

Serve it straight from the pan with crusty bread for mopping up every last drop.

Garlic Shrimp and Tomato Skillet

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 12 min · Total: 22 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oil in a 10-inch skillet over medium heat. Add the garlic and cook for 1 minute, until golden at the edges.
  2. Add the tomatoes, oregano, and red pepper flakes. Cook, stirring occasionally, until the tomatoes begin to burst, 6 to 8 minutes. Press some with a spoon to make a sauce.
  3. Season the shrimp with salt and nestle them into the tomatoes. Cook for 2 minutes, turn, and cook 1 to 2 minutes more, until pink and opaque.
  4. Sprinkle with herbs and squeeze over the lemon. Serve with bread.

Tips for success

  • Add a crumble of feta cheese at the end for a Greek-style twist.
  • Spoon it over rice or orzo for a heartier meal.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 2 days. Reheat gently so the shrimp stay tender.
