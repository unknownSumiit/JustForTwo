Chicken Noodle Soup for Two

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

A small pot of homemade chicken noodle soup with tender chicken, carrots, celery, and egg noodles. Ready in 40 minutes.

Prep10 min
Cook30 min
Total40 min
Serves2

Homemade chicken noodle soup does not have to fill a giant pot. This small-batch version makes two big bowls, perfect for a chilly evening or when one of you is under the weather.

Poaching a chicken breast right in the broth adds flavor and keeps the meat tender.

Chicken Noodle Soup for Two

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 30 min · Total: 40 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Melt the butter in a medium saucepan over medium heat. Cook the onion, carrot, and celery until softened, about 6 minutes. Add the garlic for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the broth, chicken breast, thyme, and bay leaf. Bring to a boil, then simmer until the chicken is cooked through (165°F), about 15 minutes.
  3. Remove the chicken and shred it with two forks.
  4. Add the noodles to the pot and simmer until tender, about 7 minutes.
  5. Return the chicken, stir in the parsley, remove the bay leaf, and season to taste.

Tips for success

  • A squeeze of lemon just before serving brightens the flavor.
  • Use 1 1/2 cups of leftover cooked chicken and skip the poaching step.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days. The noodles soften as they sit; add a splash of broth when reheating.
