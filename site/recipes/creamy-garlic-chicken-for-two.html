Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

Tender chicken breasts in a creamy garlic Parmesan sauce with spinach and sun-dried tomatoes. One skillet, 30 minutes.

Prep8 min
Cook20 min
Total28 min
Serves2

This skillet dinner is rich, comforting, and quick: seared chicken simmered in a creamy garlic Parmesan sauce with sun-dried tomatoes and a handful of spinach.

Serve it over pasta, rice, or with crusty bread.

Creamy Garlic Parmesan Chicken

Prep: 8 min · Cook: 20 min · Total: 28 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Season the chicken with salt and pepper. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and cook the chicken until golden and cooked through (165°F), 5 to 6 minutes per side. Transfer to a plate.
  2. Lower the heat to medium. Melt the butter, then add the garlic and sun-dried tomatoes and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Add the broth and scrape up the browned bits. Stir in the cream and simmer gently for 2 minutes.
  4. Whisk in the Parmesan, then add the spinach and stir until wilted.
  5. Return the chicken to the skillet and spoon the sauce over it.

Tips for success

  • Pound thick chicken breasts to an even 3/4 inch so they cook evenly.
  • Half-and-half makes a lighter sauce. Simmer it gently so it does not separate.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 3 days. Reheat over low heat with a splash of milk.
