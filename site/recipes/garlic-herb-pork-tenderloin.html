Garlic Herb Pork Tenderloin

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

A juicy pork tenderloin seared and roasted with garlic and herbs. Lean, tender, and perfectly sized for two.

Prep10 min
Cook25 min
Total40 min
Serves2
Jump to Recipe

A single pork tenderloin is one of the best cuts for cooking for two: it is lean, tender, affordable, and just the right size for dinner with a little left over.

A quick sear and 15 minutes in the oven give you a golden crust and a juicy, blush-pink center.

Garlic Herb Pork Tenderloin

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 25 min · Total: 40 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F. Pat the pork dry.
  2. Mix the oil, garlic, rosemary, thyme, salt, and pepper and rub all over the pork.
  3. Heat an oven-safe skillet over medium-high heat. Sear the pork on all sides until browned, about 6 minutes total.
  4. Transfer the skillet to the oven and roast for 12 to 16 minutes, until the center reaches 145°F.
  5. Move the pork to a cutting board and let it rest for 5 minutes. Meanwhile, stir the butter and mustard into the pan juices.
  6. Slice and serve with the pan sauce spooned over.

Tips for success

  • Pork cooked to 145°F with a 3-minute rest is safe and stays juicy, with a slightly pink center.
  • Leftover slices make excellent sandwiches with a little mustard.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 3 days.
