Stuffed Bell Peppers for Two

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

Two bell peppers filled with seasoned beef, rice, and tomato, topped with melted cheese. A complete dinner for two.

Prep15 min
Cook35 min
Total50 min
Serves2
Jump to Recipe

Stuffed peppers are a colorful, complete meal in one package, and they are easy to make for just two. Each pepper half holds a savory filling of beef, rice, and tomatoes under a blanket of melted cheese.

Use leftover rice or a microwavable rice pouch to keep it quick.

Stuffed Bell Peppers for Two

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 35 min · Total: 50 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 375°F.
  2. Brown the beef and onion in a skillet over medium-high heat, about 6 minutes. Drain. Stir in the garlic, Italian seasoning, and salt.
  3. Off the heat, stir in the rice, half of the tomato sauce, and 1/4 cup of the cheese.
  4. Spread the remaining tomato sauce in a small baking dish. Arrange the pepper halves cut side up and fill with the beef mixture.
  5. Cover with foil and bake for 25 minutes. Uncover, top with the remaining cheese, and bake 10 minutes more, until the peppers are tender and the cheese bubbles.

Tips for success

  • Cutting the peppers in half lengthwise helps them cook faster and makes them easier to eat.
  • Make a double batch and freeze two halves for another night.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.
