Mini Chicken Pot Pies for Two

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

Two individual chicken pot pies with a creamy filling and a flaky puff pastry top. Comfort food, perfectly portioned.

Prep15 min
Cook25 min
Total40 min
Serves2

Individual pot pies are a cozy, special dinner for two. A creamy filling of chicken and vegetables bakes in two oven-safe bowls under a golden puff pastry lid.

Using leftover or rotisserie chicken and frozen vegetables keeps it simple.

Mini Chicken Pot Pies for Two

Prep: 15 min · Cook: 25 min · Total: 40 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 400°F.
  2. Melt the butter in a saucepan over medium heat and cook the onion until soft, about 4 minutes. Stir in the flour and cook for 1 minute.
  3. Slowly whisk in the broth and milk. Simmer, stirring, until thickened, about 3 minutes. Stir in the thyme, salt, and pepper.
  4. Fold in the chicken and frozen vegetables. Divide between two oven-safe bowls or large ramekins (about 12 ounces each).
  5. Cut two rounds of puff pastry slightly larger than the bowls. Lay them on top, press the edges to the rims, and cut a small slit in each. Brush with egg.
  6. Set on a baking sheet and bake for 20 to 25 minutes, until puffed and deep golden.

Tips for success

  • Wrap and freeze the leftover puff pastry for another pot pie night.
  • Let the pies cool for 5 minutes. The filling is very hot.

Storing and reheating

Best freshly baked. Refrigerate leftovers for up to 2 days and reheat at 350°F.
