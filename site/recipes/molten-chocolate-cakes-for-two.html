Molten Chocolate Cakes for Two

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

Two rich chocolate cakes with warm, gooey centers, baked in ramekins in 12 minutes. A special-occasion dessert made easy.

Prep10 min
Cook12 min
Total22 min
Serves2
Jump to Recipe

Molten chocolate cakes are the ultimate dessert for two: rich, dramatic, and much easier than they look. The outside bakes into a tender cake while the center stays warm and saucy.

Serve them straight from the ramekins or turned out onto plates with a scoop of vanilla ice cream.

Molten Chocolate Cakes for Two

Prep: 10 min · Cook: 12 min · Total: 22 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the oven to 425°F. Butter two 6-ounce ramekins and dust with cocoa powder. Place on a baking sheet.
  2. Melt the butter and chocolate together in the microwave in 20-second bursts, stirring until smooth.
  3. In a bowl, whisk the egg, egg yolk, sugar, and salt until pale and slightly thick, about 1 minute.
  4. Whisk in the chocolate mixture, then fold in the flour.
  5. Divide between the ramekins and bake for 11 to 13 minutes, until the edges are firm but the centers still jiggle.
  6. Let stand for 1 minute. Run a knife around the edges and turn out onto plates, or serve in the ramekins.

Tips for success

  • Make ahead: fill the ramekins, cover, and refrigerate up to 1 day. Add 2 minutes to the baking time.
  • These cakes have soft, underbaked centers. If you prefer a fully baked cake, bake 3 to 4 minutes longer.

Storing and reheating

Best served warm, right out of the oven.
