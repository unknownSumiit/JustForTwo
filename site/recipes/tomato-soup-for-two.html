Creamy Tomato Soup for Two

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026

A quick, velvety tomato soup made from one can of tomatoes. Perfect with a grilled cheese sandwich.

Prep5 min
Cook20 min
Total25 min
Serves2

This silky tomato soup is made from one can of tomatoes and pantry staples, so it is easy to make any time. It is smooth, rich, and so much better than canned soup.

Pair it with a grilled cheese sandwich for a classic lunch for two.

Creamy Tomato Soup for Two

Prep: 5 min · Cook: 20 min · Total: 25 min · Serves: 2

Servings
2 servings

When you change the servings, pan sizes and cooking times may need adjusting.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat the butter and oil in a saucepan over medium heat. Cook the onion until soft, about 5 minutes. Add the garlic for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the tomatoes with their juices, the broth, sugar, and basil. Break up the tomatoes with a spoon and simmer for 12 minutes.
  3. Blend until smooth with an immersion blender, or carefully in a countertop blender with the lid vented.
  4. Stir in the cream, season with salt and pepper, and heat gently.

Tips for success

  • Top each bowl with a few croutons or a swirl of cream.
  • Fire-roasted tomatoes give the soup a gentle smoky flavor.

Storing and reheating

Refrigerate for up to 5 days. Freeze without the cream for up to 3 months.
