Welcome to Just Two Plates

Just Two Plates is a recipe site for couples and anyone cooking for two. After years of cooking for a full table, it can be hard to find recipes that do not leave you eating the same casserole for a week. Every recipe here makes two servings, with ingredients you can buy in small amounts.

We designed every page to be comfortable to read on a laptop or desktop screen. Recipes are written in large print with numbered steps, and each one has a Print Recipe Card button that prints just the ingredients and instructions, usually on a single page. You can add it to your own recipe binder.

Not sure what to cook this week? Our weekly menu lays out seven meals with a shopping list you can print or check off as you shop.
