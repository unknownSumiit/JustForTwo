How this plan works

The week starts with a pork tenderloin, and a few leftover slices make sandwiches to go with Monday’s tomato soup. Tuesday’s salmon and Friday’s shrimp scampi are both ready in under half an hour. Lemons, garlic, and parsley carry through several dinners, so very little goes to waste.

Every recipe makes two servings. Cooking for one? Tap 1 serving on each recipe card. For more ideas, read Cooking for Two: 10 Tips.
