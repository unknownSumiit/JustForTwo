About Us

Just Two Plates is a home for easy recipes sized for two, written clearly and printed big.

Our story

We started Just Two Plates because so many recipe websites are hard to read: tiny print, endless scrolling, and recipes that would not print properly. We wanted a place where the recipes we grew up with, and still cook every week, are easy to read on a laptop screen and easy to print for the kitchen counter or a recipe binder.

What makes our recipes different

  • Printable recipe cards. Every recipe has a Print Recipe Card button that prints only the ingredients, steps, and tips on a clean page.
  • Large, clear print. Our pages use large type with strong contrast, and the Text size button makes it even bigger.
  • Everyday ingredients. Our recipes use ingredients you can find in any American grocery store.
  • Made for two. Every recipe makes two servings, and you can switch to one or four with one tap.
  • Food safety first. We include safe internal temperatures and follow USDA guidance in our cooking and storage advice.

Who we are

Just Two Plates is written and maintained by the Just Two Plates team, home cooks who believe a good meal shared around the table is one of life’s great pleasures. We test our recipes in ordinary home kitchens with ordinary equipment.

Advertising

To keep Just Two Plates free for everyone, we display advertisements from Google and other partners. Advertisers do not influence our recipes or articles, and ads are never included in our printable recipe cards. Read more in our Privacy Policy.
