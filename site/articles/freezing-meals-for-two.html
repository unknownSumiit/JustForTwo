How to Freeze Meals for Two (and Actually Use Them)

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026 · 5 min read

Cooking a little extra and freezing it in two-serving portions means a homemade dinner is always on hand. Here is how to do it well.

Freeze in portions

  • Use containers or freezer bags that hold exactly two servings.
  • Freeze soups flat in bags so they stack neatly and thaw quickly.
  • Wrap individual portions of cooked meat, like taco meat, in labeled packets.

Label everything

Write the name and date on every container. Keep a simple list on the freezer door so you remember what is inside.

How long frozen food keeps its best quality

FoodBest quality
Soups and stews2 to 3 months
Cooked chicken and meat2 to 6 months
Uncooked steaks and chops4 to 12 months
Pancakes and baked goods2 to 3 months

Food kept at 0°F stays safe indefinitely, but quality declines over time. Times are based on USDA guidance.

Thaw and reheat safely

  • Thaw in the refrigerator overnight, never on the counter.
  • Reheat leftovers to 165°F.
  • Cool cooked food and refrigerate within 2 hours of cooking.

What freezes well from our recipes

Chicken noodle soup (without the noodles), tomato soup (before adding cream), stuffed peppers, and pancakes all freeze beautifully.
