The Pantry for Two: Staples for Quick Weeknight Dinners

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026 · 4 min read

With a few staples on hand, you can make a satisfying dinner for two any night without a special trip to the store. Here is our list.

Pantry

  • Pasta (linguine, penne) and rice
  • Canned whole tomatoes and tomato sauce
  • Low-sodium chicken broth (small cartons are handy)
  • Olive oil, vegetable oil, honey, and low-sodium soy sauce
  • Flour, sugar, brown sugar, baking powder, cocoa powder, oats
  • Onions and garlic

Spices

Salt, pepper, garlic powder, Italian seasoning, dried thyme, chili powder, cumin, smoked paprika, cinnamon, and red pepper flakes.

Refrigerator

  • Butter, eggs, milk, and plain Greek yogurt
  • Parmesan and cheddar cheese
  • Lemons, which brighten almost any dish

Freezer

  • Peeled shrimp, salmon fillets, and chicken breasts
  • Frozen peas, mixed vegetables, and berries
  • Puff pastry for mini pot pies

Dinners from the pantry

With these on hand, you can make shrimp scampi, tomato soup, scrambled eggs on toast, or a chocolate mug cake at a moment’s notice.
