Safe Cooking Temperatures: A Quick Reference

By the Just Two Plates team · Updated November 26, 2026 · 3 min read

A food thermometer is the only reliable way to know that meat, poultry, and seafood are safely cooked. Keep this chart handy.

FoodSafe internal temperature
Chicken and turkey (all cuts, including ground)165°F
Ground beef, pork, and lamb160°F
Beef, pork, and lamb steaks, chops, and roasts145°F, then rest 3 minutes
Fish and shellfish145°F, or until opaque and flaky
Egg dishes160°F
Leftovers and casseroles165°F

Source: USDA Food Safety and Inspection Service.

How to check

  • Insert the thermometer into the thickest part, away from bone and fat.
  • For thin cutlets like chicken piccata, insert it from the side.
  • Wash the probe in hot, soapy water after each use.

Keep it cold

Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours. Keep your refrigerator at 40°F or below and your freezer at 0°F.
